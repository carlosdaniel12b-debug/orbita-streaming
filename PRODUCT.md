# Órbita Streaming — Product Specification

## 1. Identity & Proposition
**Órbita Streaming** is a premium digital entertainment platform and service aggregator in Latin America (Ecuador, Colombia, Mexico, Chile, Peru, and international). It offers 100% private streaming profiles with personal 4-digit PIN security, Ultra HD 4K HDR quality, 24/7 continuous support, and guaranteed instant delivery in under 5 minutes via WhatsApp.

### Core Value Proposition
- **Individual Platforms:** Fixed rate of **$3.00 USD / month** per screen/profile with private PIN.
- **Combo Dúo:** Any **2 platforms for $5.00 USD / month** (save $1/mo every month) + promotional **Spotify Premium 1 month free**.
- **Pack Trío:** Any 3 platforms for **$8.00 USD / month**.
- **Canva Pro Annual:** Full 365-day access activated directly to the customer's email for **$4.00 USD / year**.
- **Instant Human Service:** Immediate WhatsApp activation (+593 99 822 6756), multiple local/crypto payment gateways (Nequi, Daviplata, Bancolombia, MercadoPago, SPEI/OXXO, Binance USDT, Zelle, PayPal).

## 2. Target Audience
1. **Streaming Enthusiasts & Cinephiles:** Looking for 4K Dolby Vision/Atmos releases across Netflix, Max, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+, and Paramount+ without paying $15-$20/mo each.
2. **Sports Fans:** Wanting live UEFA Champions League, Premier League, F1, and tennis through Disney+ (ESPN) or Mexican soccer through ViX Premium.
3. **Anime Lovers:** Fans following weekly simulcasts on Crunchyroll and Netflix.
4. **Students, Creators & Entrepreneurs:** Needing Canva Pro for design and Spotify for continuous music.
5. **Families:** Needing safe, PIN-protected profiles for children (Disney+, Nickelodeon on Paramount+).

## 3. Key Surfaces & Features
1. **Cinematic Hero:** WebGL Three.js interactive concentric orbital system with gravitational core and platform satellites.
2. **Streaming Releases & Catalog Carousel:** Curated real-world movies and series (2024-2026) organized by categories (Estrenos Recientes, Películas Populares, Series Populares, Tendencias, Recomendadas, Próximamente) and filterable by platform.
3. **Platform Visual Showcase:** Dedicated space for each platform (Netflix, Disney+, Max, Prime, Apple TV+, Paramount+, Crunchyroll, ViX) updating dynamically with animated transitions.
4. **AI Entertainment Assistant ("Orbit"):** Intelligent conversational agent recommending exact titles with visual cards (poster, genre, platform, synopsis) and guidance on plans/combos.
5. **Interactive Void Raider Arcade:** High-performance 60 FPS space shooter with player-controlled firing (WASD/mouse/space on PC, touch buttons on mobile).
6. **Transparent Pricing & Comparison:** Clear comparison between Órbita's rates and standard retail prices.
7. **Step-by-step Onboarding:** Fast 3-step activation workflow.
8. **Trust Elements & Social Proof:** Real subscriber testimonials, active server telemetry status, FAQs, and WhatsApp quick chat.
